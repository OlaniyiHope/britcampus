ABOUT BRITISH POLY

Welcome to the British Transatlantic Polytechnic (British Poly). British Poly is a cutting-edge higher institution that is building workforce that would impact positively on jobs, employment, industrial expansion and creating a better future for the people through world class polytechnic education and technological innovation.

British Poly is approved by the Federal Ministry of Education and recognised by the National Board for Technical Education in Nigeria. British Poly is located in Akure, the capital city of Nigeria’s Sunshine State, Ondo.
